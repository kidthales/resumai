# Principal Technical Consultant & Fractional Architect Sample

## Profile Overview

A principal-level software architect and hands-on technical consultant who partners with engineering leaders, studios, and growth-stage organizations to solve high-risk architectural bottlenecks, lead greenfield platform builds, and accelerate product delivery. Combines deep systems engineering expertise with pragmatic delivery management, rapid codebase audits, and team upskilling.

## Core Technical Competencies

- **Systems & Application Architecture:** Rust, TypeScript, PHP 8+, Go, C++, Python, Tauri, Godot, High-Performance Desktop & Web Applications, Distributed Backend Services
- **Infrastructure & Delivery:** Docker, CI/CD Automation, PostgreSQL, Redis, Event-Driven Systems, Cloud & Local-First Architectures, Performance Profiling
- **Consulting & Advisory Capabilities:** Technical Due Diligence, Legacy Modernization, Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), Rapid Prototyping, Cross-Functional Team Mentorship

## Strategic & Client Impact

- Rapidly audits complex codebases to isolate reliability, latency, and developer-velocity bottlenecks, delivering actionable modernization roadmaps within days.
- Architects and ships production-ready vertical slices, custom tooling, and core engine/backend systems with minimal external dependencies.
- Bridges executive business objectives and day-to-day engineering execution, establishing clean testing, Git workflow, and code quality standards that outlast the engagement.

## Target Roles & Alignment

- Principal Technical Consultant / Fractional CTO / Principal Systems Architect / Contract Lead Engineer
- B2B technical consulting, high-velocity product & game studios, architecture rescue & greenfield systems engineering
