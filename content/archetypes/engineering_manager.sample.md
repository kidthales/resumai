# Engineering Manager Sample

## Profile Overview

A servant leader and people manager focused on building, scaling, and empowering high-performing software engineering teams. Combines strong technical foundation with excellence in people development, performance management, cross-functional stakeholder alignment, and agile delivery execution.

## Core Management & Leadership Competencies

- **People Leadership:** Career development, 1-on-1 coaching, performance management, hiring & talent acquisition, engineering culture
- **Delivery & Execution:** Agile / Scrum / Kanban methodologies, roadmap planning, sprint cadence, sprint risk mitigation, release management
- **Stakeholder Collaboration:** Product management partnership, executive reporting, business requirement negotiation, resource budgeting
- **Technical Strategy:** Engineering health metrics (DORA metrics), developer productivity, technical debt management, process optimization

## Strategic & Organizational Impact

- Scales engineering teams across distributed locations while sustaining high retention, morale, and psychological safety.
- Partners with Product, Design, and Executive leadership to align technical execution with quarterly and annual business goals.
- Establishes performance evaluation frameworks, promotion ladders, and technical onboarding programs.

## Target Roles & Alignment

- Engineering Manager / Software Engineering Director / Head of Engineering
- Rapidly growing engineering teams, engineering transformation initiatives, matrixed product organizations
